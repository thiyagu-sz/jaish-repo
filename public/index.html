About the project

ResearchAI is a prototype research discovery assistant designed to help students and researchers find relevant research, understand existing studies, identify potential research gaps, and explore new research directions.

The larger research concept it comes from - a Multi-Agent Research Innovation Discovery System - combines large language models, retrieval-augmented generation, knowledge graphs and multi-agent systems into a single pipeline for research discovery and proposal writing.

Large Language Models Retrieval-Augmented Generation Knowledge Graphs Multi-Agent Systems
This prototype implements a simplified version of the proposed system. It covers paper search and AI-assisted reading only. The agent framework, vector database and knowledge graph described in the specification are future architecture, not part of this MVP.

AI-generated research insights should be verified against the original research.
